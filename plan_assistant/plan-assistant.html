1 · Overview

Purpose and scope

Plan Assistant turns the member home page into a conversation. Instead of searching through menus, a member simply asks — about eligibility, premiums, coverage, benefits or plan documents — and can act on the answer, such as paying a premium, without leaving the chat.

How a conversation starts

  1. Type any question in the “Ask a question…” box.
  2. Tap a suggested question — same as typing it.
  3. Open a card — the premium alert or an inbox message — and chat opens with that context already attached.

Visitors who have not signed in can ask general plan questions; anything about an account asks them to sign in first, then continues.

In scope

  • Four agents: Check Balance, Policy Q&A, Explain Premium, Pay Premium
  • Premium payment with the member’s explicit consent
  • Feedback, preferences and continuous improvement

Out of scope

  • Binding eligibility or claims decisions — the assistant explains the rules, it does not decide
  • Card data or sensitive health details in AI memory

4 · User journey

Swimlane — general Plan Assistant flow

This is the standard journey when a member asks the Plan Assistant for help. The same flow supports balance, policy, premium explanation and other plan-related questions.

01

Member asks

The member types a question, selects a suggested question or opens a home-screen card.

02

Chat receives

The chat sends the message along with relevant conversation context.

03

Understand request

The system checks the member’s request and identifies what help is needed.

04

Choose specialist

The supervisor routes the request to the right agent, such as Check Balance, Policy Q&A or Explain Premium.

05

Get information

The agent retrieves approved plan information or verified account details using the required tools.

06

Respond

The assistant gives a clear answer, shows sources where relevant and offers useful next steps.

07

Continue chat

The member can ask a follow-up. The assistant keeps the relevant topic and plan-period context.

If the request involves a payment or another sensitive action, the assistant asks for confirmation and authorization before anything is executed.

4 · Payment

Payment requirements

IDPayment requirementPriority
PAY-01Payment is executed server-side by the Pay Premium agent through the transactional API, regardless of LLM output.Must
PAY-02“Pay Now” is enabled only after the member ticks the authorization for the exact amount and method shown.Must
PAY-03Every execute call carries an idempotency key; retries and resumed sessions cannot double-charge.Must
PAY-04On decline or timeout, reconcile with the provider before retrying; never infer success.Must
PAY-05Tokenized card data (PCI), step-up authentication where required; no card data in LLM memory or logs.Must
PAY-06On success: update payment record, store transaction, generate receipt, notify the member.Must

6 · First-time visitor

Simple flow — first-time visitor

A first-time visitor can ask general questions without signing in. Sign-in is required only when the visitor asks for personal or account-specific information.

01

Visit home page

The visitor sees a general welcome, sign-in option and suggested public questions.

02

Ask a question

The visitor types a question or selects a suggested question.

03

Check question type

The assistant checks whether the question is general or related to a personal account.

04

General question

For general questions, the assistant answers using public plan documents. No sign-in is needed.

05

Account question

For balance, personal premium or other account details, the assistant asks the visitor to sign in.

06

Sign in and continue

After successful sign-in, the assistant resumes the saved question in the member chat.

Before sign-in, the visitor cannot view personal account details or make a payment. Any payment still follows the normal confirmation and authorization steps.

8 · Quality

Error handling

ScenarioExpected behaviour
Ambiguous request / unknown intentAsk to clarify; list supported topics.
API unavailableTemporary service error; no guessed values.
One agent fails in a multi-agent turnReturn the successful part, say what failed, log it.
No policy information foundSay approved information was not found; do not speculate.
Session expiredRe-authenticate, then restore the conversation.
Payment declined / timeout / duplicateShow verified status; reconcile with Braintree before retry; check the idempotency key.
8 · Quality

Non-functional and security requirements

IDAreaRequirement
NFR-01InputPrompt-injection screening, PII minimization, identity and intent validation.
NFR-02RetrievalApproved sources only, member-level authorization, provenance, citation checks.
NFR-03ToolsAllowlist, least-privilege scopes, schema validation, rate limits.
NFR-04OutputGrounded facts only; no fabricated balances; safe errors; redaction.
NFR-05Session and stateAuthenticated principal, expiry, inactivity timeout; durable checkpoints per thread_id; member isolation.
NFR-06MemoryConversation memory: Keep recent messages and the current task context during a chat, including the topic, plan period and any pending confirmation, so follow-up questions make sense. When the member returns, restore the saved conversation history and its state. Longer-term memory is limited to preferences the member has chosen to save, such as language or notification choices. Do not store card details, payment credentials or sensitive health information in AI memory.
NFR-07AutomationNo human-in-the-loop; sensitive actions gated by deterministic checks and the member’s own authorization.
NFR-08OperationsEncrypted storage, audit log, monitoring, abuse detection, p95 latency and token-cost tracking.
